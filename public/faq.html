Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about ProPredict
Account & Registration
How do I create an account?

Click "Create Account" in the top right corner. Enter your email and a strong password. You'll receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account. That's it!

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click "Login" then "Forgot password?" Enter your email address and we'll send you a password reset link. The link expires in 1 hour for security.

Can I change my email address?

Currently, email changes require support assistance. Contact propredict.support@gmail.com with subject "Change Email" and we'll help you update it securely.

Is email verification required?

Yes. All accounts must verify their email address before gaining full access. This prevents spam and protects your account security.

Billing & Subscriptions
What payment methods do you accept?

We accept NGN (Paystack - cards, bank transfer, USSD), USD/GBP (bank transfer), and crypto (USDC, USDT). NGN payments activate instantly; others require manual verification (within 24h).

Can I get a refund?

No. All subscription payments are final and non-refundable. This is clearly stated in our Refund Policy. You may cancel anytime to stop future billing.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Log into your dashboard, go to Account Settings, and click "Manage Subscription" then "Cancel Subscription." You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

Can I switch from monthly to yearly?

Yes! Cancel your current plan and subscribe to the new plan. Your new plan will start immediately, and you'll receive a prorated credit for unused time on your previous plan.

Why was my payment declined?

Common reasons: insufficient funds, bank restrictions on online payments, or incorrect card details. Try a different payment method or contact your bank.

Features & Usage
What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free users get 3 picks/day. Pro users get unlimited picks, elite selections, team analysis, accumulator builder, market options, and performance tracking. See our Pricing page for full comparison.

How accurate are the predictions?

Our AI achieves 65-75% accuracy on average, but NO prediction is guaranteed. Sports are unpredictable. Use our predictions as entertainment and guidance, not financial advice.

How do I use the Accumulator Builder?

Click "Add" on any pick to add it to your accumulator. The builder calculates combined probability and risk level. You can also use "Smart Acca" for AI-generated combinations.

What sports do you cover?

Football (Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, UCL), Basketball (NBA, EuroLeague), NFL, NHL, MLB, Tennis, Rugby League, Rugby Union, Darts, and Table Tennis.

How does the referral program work?

Share your unique referral link. When a friend signs up and verifies their email, you BOTH get 7 days of Pro access FREE. Track referrals in your dashboard.

Technical Support
The site isn't loading properly. What should I do?

Try clearing your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete), disabling ad blockers, or using a different browser. Check our Status page for any ongoing issues.

Is ProPredict mobile-friendly?

Yes! ProPredict is fully responsive and works on all devices. A dedicated mobile app is in development (ETA June 2026).

How do I report a bug?

Use the feedback button (bottom right) or email propredict.support@gmail.com with "Bug Report" in the subject. Include screenshots if possible.

Do you have an API?

Not yet, but a public API is on our roadmap for Q4 2026. Developers will be able to access predictions programmatically.

Still have questions?

We're here to help! Reach out to our support team and we'll respond within 24 hours.
