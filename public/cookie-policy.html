What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to the website owners.

Cookies may be "persistent" (remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser).

ProPredict uses cookies solely to improve your experience. We never use cookies to track you across other websites or sell your data to advertisers.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services we use to enhance ProPredict:

  • Paystack — Payment processing. Paystack uses cookies for fraud prevention and security.
  • Tawk.to — Live chat support. Tawk.to uses cookies to maintain chat sessions.
  • Google Fonts — Font delivery. Google may collect anonymous usage data.

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. We encourage you to review them.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Set cookies to expire when you close the browser
  • Receive warnings before cookies are placed

Chrome

Settings → Privacy → Cookies

Firefox

Options → Privacy → Cookies

Safari

Preferences → Privacy → Cookies

Edge

Settings → Cookies → Manage

Important: Blocking essential cookies may prevent you from logging in or using core features of ProPredict.

Legal Basis (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we rely on the following legal bases for using cookies:

  • Legitimate Interest — Essential cookies required for the website to function.
  • Consent — Analytics and functional cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time.
