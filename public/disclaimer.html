ProPredict strongly advocates for and encourages responsible gambling practices. If you choose to engage in sports betting or gambling activities, we urge you to follow these responsible gambling principles:

  • Avoid Chasing Losses: Accept losses as part of the gambling experience. Do not attempt to recover losses by increasing wager amounts.
  • Set Time Limits: Establish and adhere to time limits for gambling sessions. Take regular breaks and engage in other leisure activities.
  • Avoid Impaired Gambling: Do not gamble when under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or when experiencing emotional distress, fatigue, or depression.
  • Keep Gambling Recreational: View gambling strictly as a form of paid entertainment, not as a source of income, a financial strategy, or a solution to financial problems.
  • Monitor Your Behavior: Be aware of signs that gambling may be becoming problematic, including preoccupation with gambling, lying about gambling activities, or borrowing money to gamble.

If you believe that gambling is negatively affecting your life or the lives of those around you, please seek help immediately. Resources for problem gambling vary by country.
